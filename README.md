## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/-01-0D1117?style=flat-square&color=58A6FF" height="20"/> About Me

B.Tech CSE student at **SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai**, building a multidisciplinary portfolio across **AI/ML, data science, cloud, cybersecurity, full stack development, UI/UX, graphic design, motion graphics, and video editing**. I like turning ideas into real, working products — and backing that up with hands-on certifications along the way.

**Currently building**

- 🤟 **Sign2Speech** — a PyQt6-based ASL gesture recognition desktop app with a Windows 11-inspired glassmorphism dark theme, MVVM architecture, and a CNN+LSTM model targeting 100 common ASL gestures. Built with a four-person student team as a portfolio-first project, with a possible arXiv/Medium publication in the works.
- 💰 **ExpenseFlow** — a comprehensive Excel-based financial management application built with VBA, Power Query, and Power Pivot, following a detailed 17-phase spec.
- 🤝 **Community Skill Exchange Platform** — a planned web app for AI-based freelance team formation across India, using semantic/AI-embedding matching via the Google Gemini API, a time-based skill-barter system, and identity verification baked into the MVP.

- 💡 Always curious to explore where **tech meets design**.
- 🚀 Loves automating the boring stuff.
